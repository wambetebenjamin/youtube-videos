# Library

Source material that sits behind the publishing work. Nothing here is a deliverable — it is the raw
input: reference designs, transcripts, and notes.

```
library/
  design-references/    four Canva-developed PDF templates (re-homed from the repo root)
  transcripts/          cleaned episode transcripts used as source material for books
```

## design-references/

All four are Canva "Untitled design" exports, A4 (596 × 842 pt), authored by Wambete Benjamin, using
placeholder brands and lorem ipsum copy. They are kept as **layout references only** — no text, logo
or placeholder from them ships in any publication.

| File | Was uploaded as | Pages | What it is | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `school-prospectus-22pp.pdf` | `SCHOOL BOOK.pdf` | 22 | Image-led school prospectus: cover, at-a-glance, programmes, extra-curricular, facilities, admissions, parental involvement, dates | **Chosen** as the design reference for Book One |
| `employee-benefits-guide-10pp.pdf` | `BOOK FOR TODAY.pdf` | 10 | Employee benefits/perks guide | Rejected — corporate HR tone |
| `company-handbook-6pp.pdf` | `COMPANY HAND.pdf` | 6 | Company history / handbook, lorem ipsum body copy | Rejected — too short, no content architecture |
| `medical-booklet-6pp.pdf` | `medical book.pdf` | 6 | Clinic booklet | Rejected — clinical tone, wrong audience |

**Why the school prospectus won:** it is the only one whose structure transfers whole. A youth movement
needs exactly what a school prospectus needs — a welcome, values, what we do, what life looks like,
how to join, key dates. Its visual system (bold sans display type, numbered sections, photographs in
soft shapes, blue/orange/green blocking) reads as energetic rather than corporate, which suits
*CHRISCO Youth Aflame*. It also has the most pages, so there is more system to borrow from.

## transcripts/

`law-of-use-and-sowing-reaping-ep01.txt` — a cleaned transcript of a Jim Rohn talk on two "basic laws"
(the law of use; the law of sowing and reaping), taken from a YouTube video. ~7.7 KB.

It is the source material for `publishing/chrisco-youth-aflame/book-01-unused-is-lost/`. The book is
an original work written in our own words in response to the ideas; it is not a transcript, not a
reprint, and not an authorised edition of the talk.
