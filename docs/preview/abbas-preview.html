How to Render the MP4

Offline workaround: Stills are in out/abbas-still-*.png → use the bundled scripts/mux-abbas-video.mjs to mux a preview MP4 via the compositor's ffmpeg (no Chrome needed).
FreeVideo (GPU): Prompts in public/raw-footage/prompts/ → ./freevideo generate --prompt-file prompt.txt --out hero.mp4 → swap into public/raw-footage/ and re-render.
