### FULL VOICEOVER SCRIPT (≈2,250 words — 15 min at ~150 wpm)

**[0:00-1:30] COLD OPEN — The Table Is Still Set**
> You are not late. You are not forgotten. If today your heart feels heavy — if you woke up tired before the day began — this is for you.  
> At Abba's Table, there is always a seat. Not because you earned it, but because He set it.  
> Psalm 42:5 whispers to us today: “Why, my soul, are you downcast? Why so disturbed within me? Put your hope in God, for I will yet praise Him, my Savior and my God.”  
> Notice — the Psalmist does not hide his discouragement. He talks to his own soul. And he answers it with hope. Today, we will learn how.

**[1:30-4:30] PART 1 — Why Are You Downcast? (Understanding the Valley)**
Psalm 42 was written by the sons of Korah — worship leaders — far from home, far from the temple, in enemy territory. They remembered better days while living hard days. That is where many of you are.  
Discouragement is not your identity. It is a season. The Hebrew word for *downcast* is *shachach* — to sink down, to bow. Like shoulders that have carried too much for too long.  
Three signs you are in this valley:  
1. **Memory hurts** — verse 4: “I remember...” You replay what you lost.  
2. **Tears become food** — verse 3: “My tears have been my food day and night.” Grief becomes daily bread.  
3. **Questions grow loud** — “Where is your God?” The enemy, your own mind, even friends ask it.

God does not scold you for asking. He invites you to talk to your soul — not from your soul. There is a difference.

**[4:30-8:30] PART 2 — THREE STORIES AT THE TABLE (Biblical Witnesses Who Were Downcast Yet Lifted)**

**Story 1: Elijah under the broom tree — 1 Kings 19**
The prophet who called fire from heaven, one day later, begs God to take his life. He is exhausted, afraid, alone. God does not give him a lecture. He gives him sleep, bread, water, and a whisper. Not in the wind, not in the earthquake, not in fire — but a gentle whisper.  
*Reflection:* Your burnout does not disqualify you. Sometimes the most spiritual thing you can do is rest, eat, and listen for the whisper again.

**Story 2: David at Ziklag — 1 Samuel 30**
David weeps until he has no strength left. His men want to stone him. Everything is burned. But the text says: “David strengthened himself in the Lord his God.” How? He remembered Abba’s faithfulness. He asked for the ephod. He inquired. Discouraged, yet he turned his face, not his back.  
*Reflection:* When everyone blames you and you blame yourself, you can still strengthen yourself in God. Put your hope — not in outcome, in Him.

**Story 3: The bent woman — Luke 13:10-13**
Eighteen years bent over, unable to straighten. She came to synagogue anyway. Jesus saw her, called her, touched her — “Woman, you are freed.” Immediately she stood straight and praised God.  
*Reflection:* You may have been bent by disappointment for years — bent by loss, betrayal, chronic waiting. One call from Jesus is enough. You will yet stand straight.

**[8:30-12:00] PART 3 — PUT YOUR HOPE IN GOD (The Practice of Hope)**
The Psalmist repeats this phrase twice (v.5, v.11) — a refrain, like breathing. Hope in Hebrew is *yachal* — to wait expectantly, with tension like a rope pulled tight. Not wishful thinking. Expectant waiting.

How to talk to your soul today — 4 practices:

1. **Name it honestly:** “My soul, you are downcast because...” God can handle truth.
2. **Remember His track record:** Write down 3 “Ebenezers” — times He carried you before.
3. **Speak forward:** “I WILL yet praise Him.” Not “I feel like.” Praise is a decision before it is a feeling.
4. **Stay at the table:** Don’t isolate. The discouraged soul lies to you that you are alone. At Abba’s Table, discouragement is disarmed by presence.

**[12:00-14:30] PART 4 — I WILL YET PRAISE HIM (For the One Who Waits Today)**
Maybe your story is: a job lost, a marriage strained, health failing, ministry unseen, prayers unanswered since January, or since childhood.  
Hear this: God is not measuring your faith by your feelings. He is holding you while you feel.  
Romans 15:13 says: “May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace as you trust in Him, so that you may overflow with hope by the power of the Holy Spirit.” He is the God *of* hope — its Source. He fills, you overflow.

Close your eyes with me:
> My Father, Abba, I bring my downcast soul to Your table. I have been disturbed within me. Today I choose to put my hope — again — in You. Not in change of circumstance first, but in Your presence. Lift my head. Straighten what has been bent. I will yet praise You. Amen.

**[14:30-15:30] BENEDICTION & CTA**
If this met you in the valley, stay. At Abba’s Table we return every week with a reflection for the discouraged. Share this with one person who is downcast today — you may be the hand that lifts them.  
Read Psalm 42 and 43 tonight, slowly. Talk to your soul. Type in comments: “I will yet praise Him.” Let’s agree together.  
I am not leaving the table until you know: you are seen, you are held, you will rise. Shalom.
